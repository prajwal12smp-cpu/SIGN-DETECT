Engineering Highlights

Built with Cutting-Edge AI

Designed for high-throughput, low-latency client-side video processing without expensive hardware dependencies.

MediaPipe Landmark Tracking

Detects 21 3D spatial hand coordinates with millimeter accuracy in real-time, resilient to lighting and background noise.

Scale & Position Invariance

Features are translated to relative origin coordinates, enabling reliable recognition regardless of hand distance or camera position.

Random Forest Engine

High-efficiency ensemble decision trees evaluate 42 dimensional vectors in <1ms with near zero CPU footprint.

Real-Time MJPEG Streaming

Low-latency frame generator feeds annotated bounding boxes and live prediction overlays directly into modern web browsers.

Text-To-Speech Synthesizer

Integrated Web Speech API vocalizes detected signs and accumulates full sentences for two-way conversation accessibility.

Privacy-First Edge Processing

Camera frames remain strictly on your local device stream without sending video data to third-party cloud servers.
