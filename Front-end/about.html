Our Mission

Bridging The Divide with Accessible AI

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), over 430 million people worldwide experience disabling hearing loss. While Sign Language is the foundational language of the deaf community, the vast majority of hearing individuals have never had the opportunity to learn it.

SignDetect AI was built to democratize communication by transforming standard laptop and smartphone webcams into real-time sign language translators and speech synthesizers without requiring specialized hardware or data gloves.

Global Accessibility Impact

  • Healthcare: Enabling doctors to understand basic emergency patient signs.
  • Education: Interactive learning tool for ASL students and teachers.
  • Public Services: Accessible kiosks and customer support desks.
Evolution

Development Roadmap

From proof-of-concept research to an enterprise-ready assistive communication platform.

Phase 1: Foundation

Completed

Core ML pipeline & static dataset creation.

  • 14 static gesture classes
  • MediaPipe landmark extraction
  • Random Forest model training

Phase 2: Live Studio

Current Release

Real-time web application & audio synthesis.

  • Live 30 FPS MJPEG camera feed
  • Sentence builder & history feed
  • Web Speech API (TTS vocalization)

Phase 3: Deep Dynamic

Planned

Temporal sequence models for motion signs.

  • LSTM / Transformer temporal models
  • 100+ comprehensive sign vocabulary
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) offline

Phase 4: Two-Way AR

Future

Bidirectional speech-to-sign visual avatars.

  • 3D animated avatar signing speech
  • WebXR & smart glasses integration
  • Community gesture dataset hub
