Quick Reference

Supported Signs Reference

Position your hand clearly within the camera frame for optimal accuracy.

👋HELLO

Open palm raised with spread fingers facing camera.

👍YES

Closed fist with upright thumb extended.

🙅NO

Index and middle finger tips snapping to thumb.

🙏THANKS

Flat hand starting near chin moving outward.

🙇SORRY

Closed fist gently circling near the chest.

🤲PLEASE

Flat open palm making circular motion on chest.

🅰️Letter A

Fist closed with thumb pointing straight up.

🅱️Letter B

Four straight fingers up, thumb folded on palm.

1️⃣Number 1

Single upright index finger with closed fist.

2️⃣Number 2

Index and middle fingers in peace (V) shape.
